Our Story

Our imagined founding story began with three students who shared an interest in improving healthcare. Lucy, Anthony, and McKayla noticed that choosing medical imaging equipment involves more than finding a machine. Customers also need clear information, dependable technology, and training.

Combining their interests in clinical care, information technology, and marketing, they created their PrizMed business concept. Their goal was to help healthcare facilities understand their equipment options while keeping quality and cost in mind.

This origin story is fictional and was created for our assignment.

Our Mission

To reduce healthcare costs through cost-effective, value-added offerings of high-quality medical imaging equipment.

Our business profile describes PrizMed as a small, privately owned, for-profit company specializing in medical imaging equipment and services.

Your Questions, Answered

Who are our customers?

Our customers include hospital systems, imaging centers, and private medical practices looking for medical imaging equipment and related services.

Can I purchase equipment on this page?

No. This page introduces our company and team for a student project. It does not process equipment purchases, payments, or actual customer inquiries.

Who helps customers learn to use this equipment?

Lucy Lefever, our Clinical Product Specialist, provides training, demonstrations, and clinical support to help medical staff understand and operate the equipment. Anthony Russell supports the technical setup, network connections, and troubleshooting.
